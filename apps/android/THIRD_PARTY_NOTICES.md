# Bundled typeface

Plus Jakarta Sans, by Tokotype, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
Five static weights (400/500/600/700/800), sourced from Google Fonts.

The OFL permits bundling in a commercial application. The only obligations are
that the font is not sold on its own and that the licence travels with it, so
the full text lives beside these files in OFL.txt and the app credits it on the
profile screen.

Devanagari is not part of this family. Any Hindi text falls through to the
platform's own fallback chain, which is why NetworkPeerType declares the family
rather than replacing the system default outright.
